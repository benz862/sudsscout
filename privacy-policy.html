Suds Scout (sudsscout.com) is a free directory of mobile auto detailing businesses across Florida, operated by SkillBinder LLC. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, and what we do with it. In short: we collect as little as we can, we never sell personal information, and this site has no accounts and no passwords.

What we collect

What you send us. If you message us through the chat on this site, submit the join or claim form, or email us, we receive what you send: your name, contact details and the contents of your message.

Listing taps. When you tap a listed business's phone number or website link, we record the tap against that listing — which business, which page, and when — so the business can see the demand its listing creates. We do not see or store your own phone number, and a tap is never a recording of a call.

Basic site analytics. We may use privacy-respecting analytics that record pages visited and general device type. We do not run advertising trackers for third parties.

How we use it

To run the directory, answer your messages, pass an enquiry to a business when you ask us to, show businesses the tracked demand for their own listing, and improve the site. That is all.

Chat and email

If you ask us in chat to email you information — for example a business's details or how to join — we will send it from this directory's own branded email. If you no longer want email from us, reply and say so, or email info@skillbinder.com, and we will stop.

Sharing

We share an enquiry with the business it concerns when you ask us to pass it on. We share information only as needed to run the site (for example with our website and CRM providers) or when the law requires it. We never sell personal information.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing info@skillbinder.com.

Contact

SkillBinder LLC, North Fort Myers, FL. Email: info@skillbinder.com — or message us any time through the chat on this site.
