Questions businesses ask

When will you offer me Featured?
When your free listing has earned it: at least 3 tracked intent taps in 30 days, or a tracked enquiry through the site. Then we email you the numbers and the offer — the decision is yours.

What counts as an intent tap?
A tap on your phone number or website link on your listing. It is proof a real visitor intended to contact you — not a guess, and not a view.

Why do listings have to be verified?
Because car owners trust a directory whose listings are real. We check your business against your own published website or page — phone, services and areas must match what you publish — before anything goes live.
